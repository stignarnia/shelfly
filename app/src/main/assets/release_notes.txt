Shelfly 4.0.5

• Opening a show or a film from a link, a notification or a widget no longer throws you back to Progress on the way. It opens over whatever you were looking at, and back returns you there.
• The search widget and the search shortcut now open search directly, without a stop on Discover first.
• The bottom bar no longer stays on top of Settings when a sync notification opens it.
• Sheets that slide up from the bottom - an actor, the links, the sort order - now follow your theme. They ignored the pure black and Material You settings and always came up in plain light or dark.
• The widgets follow your theme instead of always being dark, pure black and Material You included. They change with the app, so there is nothing to set up.
• Each widget can also be themed on its own, from its settings on the home screen: hold the widget, tap the settings button and pick a theme for that one. Material You for widgets needs Android 14.
• Widgets now change as soon as you change something, instead of waiting for a refresh that could take until the next restart. Very long lists are cut to what a widget is allowed to hold.
• Widgets need Android 12 and are no longer offered below it. The older way of filling them stopped being updated by Android, leaving widgets that could not repaint at all.
• Each widget has a transparency slider in its home screen settings to adjust its background opacity.
• The home screen widget picker now shows previews of what each widget looks like before you add it.
