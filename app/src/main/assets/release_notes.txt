Shelfly 4.0.5

• Opening a show or a film from a link, a notification or a widget no longer throws you back to Progress on the way. It opens over whatever you were looking at, and back returns you there.
• The search widget and the search shortcut now open search directly, without a stop on Discover first.
• The bottom bar no longer stays on top of Settings when a sync notification opens it.
• Sheets that slide up from the bottom - an actor, the links, the sort order - now follow your theme. They ignored the pure black and Material You settings and always came up in plain light or dark.
