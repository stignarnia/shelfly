Shelfly 4.0.1

• Fixed the label and outline of text fields in dialogs (API keys, WebDAV, backup retention) turning near-black and unreadable once the field was focused.
• Dropped the crown icon from the Theme setting. It marked a Showly premium feature that no longer exists in this fork.
• Fixed IMDb, Metascore and Rotten Tomatoes ratings never loading. Details were served from a cache entry that carried no IMDb id, so there was nothing to look up, and OMDb was queried on a host that only accepts paid keys.
• Fixed the movie Discover page failing to load entirely.
• Ratings that cannot be found now show as absent instead of spinning forever.
• With no OMDb key set, IMDb, Metascore and Rotten Tomatoes now show a cross you can tap for an explanation, instead of sitting there empty.
