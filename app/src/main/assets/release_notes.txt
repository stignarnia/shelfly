Shelfly 4.0.6

• New brand accent color across the app, widgets, and launcher icon.
• New app icon and branding.
• Opening Settings now fades in smoothly from Discover and across the app.
• Full landscape, tablet, and foldable support across all screens with dynamic rotation.
• Posters on Discover now scroll up under the header instead of vanishing at the top of the list.
• The Shows Calendar and Movies Calendar widgets now show a preview when you pick a widget, instead of failing to load.
• Widget headers are now translated instead of always appearing in English.
• Countdowns to an episode or release now read correctly in French, Portuguese, Russian, and Ukrainian.
• Small labels in lists, search, and widgets are now large enough to follow your system font size.
• Screen readers now announce the create-list and back-to-top buttons.
